What is analyzed

Only timestamps, addresses, protocols, ports, lengths, TCP flags, ICMP types, DNS query names, and an encryption estimate.

HTTPS and other encrypted payloads remain encrypted and cannot be inspected. No credentials, cookies, tokens, form fields, messages, files, or complete payloads are extracted.

Strict boundaries

No packet injection, spoofing, replay, decryption, payload reconstruction, ARP poisoning, MITM, wireless attacks, evasion, exploitation, or destructive action is implemented.

Point-in-time educational observation

Rules are transparent heuristics and may produce false positives. A short capture cannot prove that a network is secure or that an attack occurred.
