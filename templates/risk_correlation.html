How Risk Correlation Works

Transparent multi-module security correlation engine rules:

Web Exposure Correlation

Aligns open public web ports with missing security headers to calculate elevated surface exposure.

Multi-Vector Risk Elevation

Elevates risk to High/Critical when perimeter exposures align with suspicious DPI traffic indicators.

Confidence Weighting

Adjusts correlation confidence higher for corroborated clusters and lower for isolated observations.

Attack Path Generation

Synthesizes plausible attacker pathways from external exposure to potential compromise surface.

Methodological Scope and Educational Boundaries

The Cyber Risk Correlation Engine operates strictly on observational findings generated by authorized local scans. It performs no active exploitation, fuzzing, or payload decryption. Results represent potential attack vectors and point-in-time risk correlations requiring investigation.
